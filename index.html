AboutResearchWriting

I currently do applied ML at eBay. My focus has mostly been on evals: understanding the failure modes of ML and LLM applications, and where they can go haywire. I build strategy around model retraining and help Product and Applied Sciences build good, reliable and scalable ML models.

Outside work, I enjoy doing independent research. I've been a big fan of independent research since the day I found out it can be a serious thing. :) Really, I was shocked. I'm passionate about it for two reasons. First, it gives me freedom of thought, which lets me pursue the wide variety of ideas I want to work on, so it's liberating. Second, it's fast-paced and not slowed down by institutional norms.

Right now I'm intrigued by recursive AI: what it means, how we're going to achieve it, what it would mean to achieve it, and what it could mean for humanity. You'll find my thinking on it in Writing and Research.

I'm also really intrigued by inference at scale, though I haven't had much time to work on it lately. The idea is how to optimize for low latency and low cost when serving LLMs and VLMs.

I enjoy working out a lot, eating healthy, binge-watching, arguing with my girlfriend, and sometimes my failed attempts at making music. That's pretty much me.
